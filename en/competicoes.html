Competitions

By Uncle Predictor · Published on 30 July 2026 · Updated on 9 August 2026

A points game - no money betting. Uncle Predictor involves no money, takes no deposits and pays out no cash prizes. Nothing on this page is betting advice.

Each Uncle Predictor competition is an independent game, with its own leaderboard and its own roll of honour. You can be signed up to several at the same time and switch between them - your predictions in one don't interfere with the others.

2026/27 season competitions

Unlike a month-long tournament, a domestic league runs across the whole season. That changes the nature of the game: instead of a four-week sprint, it's a marathon of dozens of matchdays where consistency counts as much as the occasional gamble.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿 Premier League

The English league, matchday by matchday, from August to May. Twenty teams, 38 matchdays, everyone playing everyone home and away.

Historically the most unpredictable of the big leagues - which, in scoring terms, is good news: more unpredictability means more rarity bonus opportunities for anyone willing to risk results the majority won't. It's also the competition with the most matches and therefore the one that most rewards never missing a deadline: one forgotten matchday in a long league costs far more in the final leaderboard than in a short tournament.

⭐ Champions League

The Champions League in its single-league format with 36 teams, where each team plays eight matches against eight different opponents, followed by the knockout rounds. The league phase draw is in August and the matches begin in September.

The league format with different opponents for each team creates matches between clubs of very different levels - and, with them, wide scorelines that are hard to call exactly. It's the competition where the Joker decides the most.

🌍 World Cup

The tournament that gave birth to the game, and where its mechanics were designed: a group stage, a knockout stage with boosted scoring, near misses, and a season picks form worth a significant slice of the total.

The 2026 edition has now finished.

How to join a competition

  1. Create an account at unclepredictor.pt (email and password or a Google account).
  2. In the competition selector, pick the ones that interest you and join.
  3. Fill in that competition's season picks before the first match, if it has them.
  4. From there, predict match by match. Each prediction closes 1 hour before kick-off.
Joining mid-way is possible - a competition that's underway accepts new entries as long as there are matches left to predict. But matches already played can't be recovered, and season picks close right at the start. Anyone joining a league mid-way starts behind on points.

Private leagues within each competition

In any competition you can create a private league and play only against your own group, with a separate table. You predict once and that prediction counts towards the overall leaderboard and every private league you're in. See private leagues.

Calendars follow each competition's official dates and are synchronised automatically. Schedule changes by the organising bodies are reflected in the game.
