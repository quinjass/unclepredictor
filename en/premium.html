Uncle Predictor Premium

Updated on 10 August 2026

The game is free and stays free. Premium adds analysis and convenience - never a competitive edge. No premium feature grants points, Jokers or bonuses that other participants can't have.

€2.99 for 3 months or €6.99 for the Season Pass. The pass is time-based and valid in every competition. One-off payment, no subscription and no automatic renewal.

The principle: no sporting advantage

This is the rule that defines everything else, and it will not change: Premium cannot alter your score or anyone else's. It gives no extra points, no extra Jokers, doesn't change the rarity bonus and gives no access to match information that free users don't have.

The reason is simple: a leaderboard where paying players score more becomes worthless. What Premium sells is time, analysis and convenience - not results.

The features in detail

Insights before every match

Before you lock in your prediction, a summary of what's publicly known about that match: what the bookmakers are saying, both teams' recent form, the head-to-head record and where the score the community seems to be underestimating lies.

It's the difference between predicting off the top of your head and predicting after taking a look. It doesn't give you points - it gives you context to decide better.

Fairness note: public, aggregated information only. It never shows other participants' individual predictions.

Predictions up to kick-off

Without Premium, your prediction closes one hour before the match starts. With Premium, it closes at kick-off. You gain that hour - the one that usually brings the starting line-ups and the last-minute news - and you stop missing matches because you remembered too late.

Nobody predicts while the match is under way: in both cases the deadline closes before the ball starts rolling.

Advanced personal stats

A chart of your position matchday by matchday, your hit rate broken down by type (exact score, outcome, near miss), performance by competition stage and head-to-head against any other participant. The data already exists - Premium gives you the read on it.

Your prediction's rarity before the deadline

Today you see your prediction's rarity after the match closes. With Premium you see, before it closes, what percentage of the community wrote the same score as you - which turns the rarity bonus into an informed decision instead of a guess.

Fairness note: this shows the aggregate - the percentage -, never other participants' individual predictions. Those still become visible to everyone at the same time, and to no one before that.

More private leagues

Free lets you be in 5 private leagues per competition; Premium, in 15. Enough for anyone who runs leagues for several groups at once. See private leagues.

No ads

The game shows discreet ads to free users - never in the prediction flow or on the live screen, the two moments when you're playing for real. Premium removes them entirely.

What is NOT premium

It's worth spelling out what stays free, because it's a lot: predicting in every competition, the Joker, the rarity bonus, the leaderboard, the honours, private leagues, live scores, notifications, the knockout simulator and the shareable matchday card. None of that is paid, and none of it ever will be.

Invite friends

You can get Premium without spending money. If you invite participants who genuinely stick around and play, you earn passes when you reach certain milestones - the pass lands in your account and activates whenever you choose, adding to any time you already have. The Ambassadors screen, inside the game, shows your invite link and your progress towards the next milestone.

Questions we've been asked

Does Premium give an advantage on the leaderboard?
No, and it's a structural rule, not a vague promise. No premium feature alters points, Jokers or the rarity bonus calculation.
If I don't pay, am I limited?
Not in the game itself. You predict in every competition, you have the Joker, rarity, private leagues, live scores, notifications, the simulator and the leaderboard. What you don't have is the analysis-and-convenience layer, you predict up to one hour before the match instead of up to kick-off, and you see ads.
How much does it cost?
€2.99 for 3 months, or €6.99 for the Season Pass. One-off payment, no subscription and no automatic renewal.
How long does the pass last?
Whatever you bought: 3 months, or until the end of the season. While it lasts, it's valid in every competition - there's no need to choose where to use it. If you renew before it runs out, the new time adds to what you still had.
When does it start?
Premium is up and running. The 2026/27 competitions are already open: the Premier League kicks off on 21 August 2026 and the Champions League on 8 September, with the league-phase draw on 27 August.
Can I buy it now?
Purchases open at the start of the season. Until then you can create an account, join the competitions and play - Premium is an extra, never a condition for playing.
