1. Nature of the service

Uncle Predictor is a recreational, social sports prediction game in which participants predict the results of football matches and are scored according to how close their predictions come to the actual results.

It is not a betting service. There is no money betting, there are no monetary prizes, there is no possibility whatsoever of financial gain or loss, and there is no relationship of any kind with gaming or betting operators. Participation is based exclusively on points and positions in leaderboards.

4. Game rules and scoring

The scoring rules in force are described in rules and scoring and form an integral part of these terms.

  • Deadlines are final. Predictions close before the start of each match and are not reopened under any circumstances. Reopening a prediction after participants' predictions have become visible would confer an unfair advantage.
  • Scoring is automatic, calculated from the results obtained by synchronisation with sports data sources.
  • If a material error is detected in the scoring or in the data of a match, the correction may be applied retroactively, including after the leaderboard has been published.
  • The rules may be adjusted between competitions, with effect for competitions that start after the change. Changes during the course of a competition are limited to error corrections.

5. Prohibited conduct

It is not permitted to:

  • Create or use multiple accounts, fake accounts or accounts on behalf of third parties.
  • Coordinate predictions between accounts with the aim of manipulating leaderboards or the calculation of the rarity bonus.
  • Attempt unauthorised access to the service, its database or the accounts of other participants.
  • Use automated means to extract data from the service, overload the infrastructure or circumvent technical limits.
  • Exploit errors or flaws in the service to gain an advantage instead of reporting them.

Non-compliance may lead to the annulment of scores, exclusion from a competition or the closure of the account.

6. Removal of participants and leaderboard integrity

A participant's rarity bonus depends on the predictions of everyone else. For that reason, removing an account with predictions already recorded in an ongoing competition would retroactively alter the scores of third parties. In those situations, deletion of the account may have to be processed only at the end of the competition, without prejudice to the rights set out in the privacy policy.
