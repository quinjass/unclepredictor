How it works

By Uncle Predictor · Published on 30 July 2026 · Updated on 9 August 2026

A points game - no money betting. Uncle Predictor involves no money, takes no deposits and pays out no cash prizes. Nothing on this page is betting advice.

You pick a competition, predict the result of every match before it kicks off, and score points based on how close you get to the real result. Whoever has the most points at the end of the competition wins. The rest of this page covers how that plays out in practice.

1. Create an account

You sign up at unclepredictor.pt, with email and password or with a Google account. You'll need to choose a nickname - it's the name you appear under in leaderboards and leagues, and it's also your login identifier.

Playing is free and there's nothing to install: the game runs in your phone or computer browser. There's also an Android app that loads the same version of the game.

2. Choose your competitions

Once you're in, the competition selector appears. Each competition is an independent game with its own leaderboard - you can be signed up to several at the same time and switch between them without losing anything.

Join the ones that interest you. A competition that's underway accepts new entries as long as there are matches left to predict, but anyone joining mid-way naturally starts behind on points. See the available competitions.

3. Fill in your season picks

Before the first match there's a form covering the whole competition: who wins the title, who makes the podium, who's the top scorer, and other predictions depending on the format. It closes before kick-off of the first match and doesn't reopen.

It's the part most people put off, and the one that decides the most competitions - it's worth a significant slice of the total, and you can complete it in fifteen minutes, one time only.

4. Predict every match

Each match has a card where you enter the result you predict - not "home win", but 2-1. Predictions close 1 hour before kick-off.

There's no save button: whatever you enter is stored automatically. The card changes colour when your prediction is registered, and you can edit it as many times as you like until the deadline.

The Joker

Every matchday you get one Joker to spend on a match of your choice, doubling the points for that match. Choosing the right match for your Joker is half the strategy - the guides have a whole article on it.

If you forget

If a match closes without a prediction from you, you inherit the AI auto-player's prediction and only the outcome counts. There's a limit per competition. It's a safety net, not an alternative to predicting.

5. Follow it live

While matches are on, scores update automatically and you can see in real time how many points you're making - and how many everyone else is making. Once a match's deadline passes, everyone's predictions become visible: you can open any match and see what each participant risked.

6. Leaderboard and rarity

The overall leaderboard adds everything up: match predictions, season picks, Jokers and rarity bonuses. Some competitions also have their own side tables, each with its own winner - they're described in each competition's rules.

Each match card also shows the rarity of your prediction - the percentage of participants who predicted the same. It's the indicator that tells you, before the match, whether you're playing it safe or taking a risk.

7. Notifications

The game keeps you from missing deadlines:

  • A daily alert when matches are closing within the next 48 hours.
  • An alert 4 hours before kick-off of a match you haven't predicted yet.
  • An in-app bell with your history of alerts and announcements.

8. Play with friends

Beyond the overall leaderboard you can create a private league - a closed group with its own table, for your family, your group of friends or your workmates. You still predict just once: the same predictions count towards the overall leaderboard and every league you're in. See private leagues.
