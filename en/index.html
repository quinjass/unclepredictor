The exact score is worth more

Getting the winner right earns you points. Getting the exact score right earns you a lot more. It's what separates the risk-takers from those who play it safe.

Joker and rarity bonus

Once per matchday you double the points on one match. And if you nail a score almost nobody else dared to pick, you earn extra points.

Private leagues

Create a closed table for friends, family or workmates. You predict once and it counts for the overall table and every league you're in.
